Air Quality Index (AQI) in Delhi

The Air Quality Index (AQI) in Delhi frequently ranges from moderate to hazardous due to industrial emissions, traffic, and seasonal crop burning. Monitoring AQI is vital for public health, especially for vulnerable groups such as children and the elderly.

This platform provides AI-powered insights and preventive recommendations based on real-time data. Stay informed. Stay safe.
